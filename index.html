What We Do

Our Core Relief Programs

Designed to reach every corner where hunger meets helplessness, ensuring proper nutrition and respect.

Daily Meal Distribution

Fresh, clean, and nutritious cooked meals packed and distributed daily to daily-wage workers and slum areas.

Ration Kit Support

Monthly grocery and dry ration kits provided to extremely poor families, widows, and elderly citizens living alone.

Emergency Food Drives

Rapid response food support teams deployed during natural calamities, cold waves, or extreme emergencies.
